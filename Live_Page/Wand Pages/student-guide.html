Color Quest

Find the colors. Beat the clock. Win!

🎮 How to Play

1
Look at your wand

The top row of lights shows the colors you need to find — in order, left to right!

2
Find the right color tag

The middle of the wand glows in the color you need next. Go find that color tag!

3
Tap it to the wand

Hold the tag flat and still on the wand's reader. Wait for the swirly animation — about 1 second.

4
Find all colors to win!

The bottom row fills in as you go. Get them all and the wand goes rainbow!

💡 Pro Tips

🚳
Hold still! Keep the tag flat and steady on the wand for one second. Don't wave it around.
👀
Check the glow! The middle of your wand shows the color you need. Use it as a hint.
💨
Wrong tag? No worries! You don't lose progress. Just find the right one and keep going.
⏱
You're being timed! The clock starts when you get the quest. Move fast!
◉
Side Button
Press it to restart the quest from the beginning. The timer resets too!
