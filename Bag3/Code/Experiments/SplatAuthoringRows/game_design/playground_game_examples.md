# Playground game examples by type

Combined and refined from three brainstorm batches (Claude, Agent A, Agent B) and their reviews.
Similar ideas are merged; each game names its sources. Ages 5-9. Every game starts with one tap
on a game card.

**Check** says how the equipment knows a child did it right. **Needs** links to the system asks at
the end; a game without it runs on the current equipment.

Speaker station abilities are not verified. Games that depend on them are marked with **S1**.

## How games are rated

**CT** names the computational thinking outcome (pattern recognition, algorithms, decomposition,
abstraction) and how strongly the game teaches it: **strong** when the outcome is the game's core
mechanic, **partial** when it is present but secondary, **weak** or **none** otherwise.

**Access** scores the game as written against ChatBroadcast `knowledge/accessibility.md`, 0-2 per
group, out of 10:

| Group | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CB** color-blind | color carries no meaning, or a shape backs it | color carries meaning; fixable with shapes or a safer pair | — |
| **BLV** blind / low-vision | playable by ear and touch | needs a sound or buzz added, or a partner | visual by design; no good fix |
| **DHH** Deaf / hard of hearing | every key sound also shows on lights or display | a key sound has no visual; fixable | — |
| **ND** neurodivergent / sensory | gentle mistakes, predictable signals, progress shown | loud, sudden or high-pressure; fixable | — |
| **Culture** | neutral, or draws on several cultures | a single-culture theme; fixable by rotating | — |

**Fix** lists the changes that raise each 1 to 2. Most are small: a shape per color, a sound per
event, a soft mistake sound.

**Not covered by the knowledge: mobility.** `accessibility.md` has no guidance for wheelchair users
or children with limited mobility. Most games here involve running or stomping. Splats can be
pressed by hand, and running games can use zones or a partner, but nothing tells the chat agent
this. Recorded as system ask **K1**.

## Type 1: one child, wand only

### Echo Beat
> "The wand buzzes a short rhythm of short and long beats. The child shakes it back the same
> way. Get it right and it plays a happy tune and gives a longer rhythm."

- **Check:** shake count and timing against the buzzed rhythm.
- **Merged from:** Secret Code Breaker, Magic Rhythm, Shake-a-Beat.
- **CT:** **Pattern recognition** (strong); decomposition (partial).
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** flash the lights with each beat for DHH.

### Traffic Light
> "The wand glows green and the child can run. It turns red at random times, and the child must
> hold perfectly still until it turns green, or it honks."

- **Check:** movement while red. Running is honor system.
- **Safety:** no shaking needed while running.
- **Needs:** A2.
- **Merged from:** Shake and Freeze, Traffic Light Freeze.
- **CT:** **Algorithms** (partial: one if-then rule).
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** add a stop/go shape (red/green is the classic color-blind pair); a distinct red buzz; a soft honk; frame it as Las Estatuas de Marfil or Red Light Green Light.

### Firefly Catch
> "The wand flashes yellow for a moment, then goes dark. Shake while it's lit to catch the
> firefly. How many can you catch?"

- **Check:** shake timing against the lit window.
- **Merged from:** Firefly Catch (B), Rainbow Shake (A, as a variant: the count of shakes picks the color).
- **CT:** Weak: reaction game, no clear CT outcome.
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** buzz while the firefly is lit.

## Type 2: one child, own 1-2 Splats

### Simon Stomp
> "The wand flashes a color pattern like red-blue-red. Stomp your two Splats in that order. The
> pattern grows each time you get it right."

- **Check:** the order of stomps against the pattern.
- **Merged from:** Simon Stomp (B), Color Trail (A), Hopscotch Builder.
- **CT:** **Pattern recognition**, **algorithms** (strong).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** give each Splat its own note as well as a color; show the pattern length as progress.

### Frog Hopper
> "The wand turns blue or green. Jump onto the Splat that matches, and keep hopping as the wand
> changes color faster."

- **Check:** the stomped Splat's color against the wand's color.
- **Merged from:** Frog Hopper (B), Color Match Stomp.
- **CT:** Pattern recognition (weak: one-step match).
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** use blue/orange, not blue/green; a note per Splat; cap the speed-up.

### Animal Piano
> "Stomp a Splat to hear a farm animal. Shake the wand to change which animal it plays, and stomp
> out your own animal song."

- **Check:** none needed; open play.
- **Merged from:** Animal Piano (B), Splat Symphony and Animal Hop (A).
- **CT:** Abstraction (weak); open creative play.
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** wand shows the animal's icon or color when it plays.

## Type 3: turns at one shared station (each tap replaces the last)

### Feelings Face
> "Tap a feeling card on the picture display to show a happy, sad or silly face. Act it out
> until the next child taps a new one."

- **Check:** the display reads the card. Acting is honor system.
- **Merged from:** Emotion Commotion (B), Picture Pop (A).
- **CT:** Abstraction (partial: a feeling as a face).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** a distinct sound per feeling and raised marks on cards; acting out is optional.

### Story Scene
> "Picture cards are hidden around the yard. Bring one to the display and tap it to change the
> character or place in our class story."

- **Check:** the display reads the card.
- **Merged from:** Story Builder, Treasure Reveal (A).
- **CT:** **Algorithms** as sequencing (partial).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 1). **Fix:** a sound per card for BLV; characters and places from several cultures.

### Giant Puzzle
> "The display shows a mixed-up picture. Each card tap turns one piece. Fix one piece and leave
> it for the next friend."

- **Check:** the display tracks the pieces and celebrates when it's solved.
- **CT:** **Pattern recognition** (strong).
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 0 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** pieces differ by shape, not only color; show pieces left. No good fix for blind children: visual by design.

### DJ Switch
> "Tap an instrument card at the music speaker. It plays only that instrument until the next
> child taps a different one."

- **Needs:** S1.
- **Merged from:** DJ Mix-Up, Musical Switch (A).
- **CT:** Decomposition (weak: one instrument at a time).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** display or wand shows the instrument icon; a volume cap; instruments from several traditions (marimba, guitarrón).

## Type 4: one wand leads, every wand follows

### Magic Freeze
> "When I shake my wand, everyone's wand buzzes and turns red, and they freeze like statues.
> When I tap my card, their wands chime green and they can move."

- **Check:** movement while red marks who moved.
- **Needs:** A2.
- **Merged from:** Magic Freeze, Freeze!, Earthquake (B, as a "drop and cover" variant).
- **CT:** **Algorithms** (partial: signal, then response).
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** soft buzz, not loud; same signal every time; frame as Las Estatuas de Marfil.

### Color Says
> "I pick a color on my wand and every wand matches it. Each color means a move: red stop, green
> wiggle, blue fly like a bird."

- **Check:** honor system.
- **Merged from:** Teacher Says, Follow the Firebird (B), Follow the Spark, Wand Parade (A).
- **CT:** **Algorithms** (strong: a rule table); abstraction (partial).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** a shape and a sound per color; frame as Simón Dice.

### Wizard Beat
> "One child is the wizard. Whatever rhythm the wizard shakes, everyone's wand pulses with it,
> and the class marches in time."

- **Check:** honor system.
- **Needs:** A5.
- **Merged from:** Magic Statues (B), Sneaky Spy Follow, Shadow Tag.
- **CT:** Pattern recognition (partial).
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** pulse by buzz as well as light.

## Type 5: everyone together, led by a speaker or display

### Musical Colors
> "Music plays while the display shows a color. When the music stops, every wand flashes a
> random color. If yours matches the screen, you win the round."

- **Check:** each wand compares its color with the broadcast color.
- **Needs:** S1 (stop/start broadcast).
- **CT:** None: the win is chance.
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** a shape per color; a sound per color; wands flash when the music stops. Consider reworking so a child chooses, or drop it.

### Action Echo
> "The speaker plays a string of funny sounds, a boing then a whistle, and the class does an
> action for each. One more sound is added every round."

- **Check:** honor system.
- **Needs:** S1.
- **Merged from:** Action Echo (B), Animal Parade, Musical Islands (B).
- **CT:** **Pattern recognition**, **algorithms** (strong: a growing sequence).
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** display shows an icon for each sound as it plays.

### Picture Moves
> "The display shows an animal or a shape. Move like the animal, or form the shape as a group,
> until the next picture appears."

- **Check:** honor system; wands chime when the picture changes.
- **Merged from:** Shape Scramble (B), Jungle Dance, Picture Freeze (A).
- **CT:** **Abstraction** (strong: a shape from bodies).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** a sound per picture; forming shapes without touching; animals from several regions.

## Type 6: teams by team card

### Treasure Hunt Race
> "Tap a red or blue team card. Find treasure cards around the playground and tap them. Each one
> chimes and counts for your team."

- **Check:** treasure card taps; the teacher's wand shows team totals.
- **Needs:** A3.
- **Merged from:** Treasure Hunt Race (B), Team Light Collector, Treasure Team Hunt (A).
- **CT:** Weak: search, no CT structure.
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** team shape as well as color; BLV children search with a partner.

### Shake Relay
> "Tap a Cats or Dogs team card and line up. When my wand signals, the first child stands still
> and shakes ten times until it chimes, then high-fives the next."

- **Check:** shake count.
- **Safety:** shaking standing still, not while running; a high-five replaces the baton.
- **Merged from:** Animal Relay (B), Relay Builders, Animal Team Relay (A).
- **CT:** Algorithms (weak: turn order).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** team shapes; flash the lights with the chime.

### Team Charge
> "Teams stand in their own zone and shake to charge their team color. The teacher's wand shows
> which team charged up first."

- **Check:** shake counts, combined per team.
- **Safety:** standing in a zone.
- **Needs:** A3.
- **Merged from:** Color Clash (B), Synchronized Shake, Red vs. Blue Shake (A).
- **CT:** None.
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** team shapes; a buzz per charge level; cap the noise.

## Type 7: closed small group from one child's wand (last resort)

### Hot Potato
> "One child taps to start and gets the hot potato: their wand flashes red. When they shake it,
> the potato jumps to a random friend's wand in the group."

- **Check:** the shake sends the potato; the wand that receives it lights up.
- **Needs:** A4.
- **Merged from:** Hot Potato Pass (B), Pass the Secret.
- **CT:** Weak.
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** buzz when the potato arrives; a gentle arrival sound; frame as papa caliente.

### Secret Agents
> "Start the spy ring and every wand in your group pulses the same secret pattern. Find your
> fellow agents by their matching lights."

- **Check:** honor system.
- **Needs:** A4.
- **Merged from:** Secret Agents (B), Secret Spark Circle, Firefly Secret (A).
- **CT:** Pattern recognition (partial: match a pattern).
- **Access:** 7/10 (CB 1 · BLV 0 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** patterns differ in timing, not color. No good fix for blind children: finding others by sight.

Dropped: Little Train and Synchronized Swimming need wand-to-wand proximity or tight cross-wand
timing (A6, A5). Moon Mission (A) is type 4.

## Type 8: closed small group at one station

### Code Breakers
> "A small group gets color cards. Tap three on the display in the right order to open the
> treasure chest."

- **Check:** the display checks the card sequence.
- **Merged from:** Code Breakers (B), Memory Picture.
- **CT:** **Algorithms** (strong: order matters); pattern recognition (partial).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** cards with shapes and raised marks; a sound per tap; show how many are right.

### Recipe Mix-Up
> "Tap ingredient cards on the display. Find the right set for a pie and the screen bakes it."

- **Check:** the display checks the card set (order free).
- **Merged from:** Recipe Mix-Up (B), Picture Puzzle Pals, Animal Match Circle (A).
- **CT:** **Decomposition** (strong: a dish as ingredients); abstraction (partial).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 1). **Fix:** raised or braille card marks; rotate dishes (pupusas, tamales, pie) instead of pie only.

### Campfire Band
> "A few children sit around the speaker's campfire crackle. Each one taps a card to add a sound,
> a howl, a drum or a boing, and together they build the scene."

- **Check:** the speaker reads the cards and layers the sounds (layering is allowed in a group).
- **Needs:** S1, S2.
- **Merged from:** Campfire Tales (B), Band Practice, Sound Band (A), Weather Station.
- **CT:** **Decomposition** (strong: parts of a whole).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** display shows an icon per sound; limit layered volume.

## Type 9: up to 4 children, up to 4 Splats, one host wand

### Pattern Stomp
> "The host flashes a sequence of colors on the four Splats. The group stomps them back in the
> same order. Each round adds one more."

- **Check:** the host checks the stomp order.
- **Merged from:** Pattern Stomp Relay (B), Simon Stomps, Rainbow Rhythm Race (A), Lilypad Leap.
- **CT:** **Pattern recognition**, **algorithms** (strong).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** a note per Splat as well as a color; show the round number.

### Whack-a-Splat
> "One Splat lights up. Stomp it before it fades. The group tries to beat its best score."

- **Check:** the host counts hits before the timeout.
- **Score:** the group's, not each child's (a Splat does not know who stomped it). Per-child
  scoring needs A1.
- **Merged from:** Whack-a-Color, Splat Sprint (A).
- **CT:** None: reaction game.
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 2 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 1 · Culture 2). **Fix:** the lit Splat also plays a sound; a gentle timeout, not a fail noise.

### Frog Pond
> "Four lilypad Splats glow green. One croaks like a frog. Hop to it and stomp it before the next
> frog calls."

- **Check:** the host checks that the croaking Splat was the one stomped.
- **Merged from:** Frog Pond (A).
- **CT:** Pattern recognition (weak: locate by sound).
- **Access:** 9/10 (CB 2 · BLV 2 · DHH 1 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** the croaking Splat also flashes. Strong for blind children: played by ear.

### Splat Chairs
> "The host wand plays music while the Splats glow green. When the music stops, one turns red.
> Everyone stomps a green one and stays off the red."

- **Check:** the host records stomps on the red Splat.
- **Merged from:** Musical Splat Chairs (B).
- **CT:** Algorithms (weak: one avoid rule).
- **Access:** 8/10 (CB 1 · BLV 1 · DHH 2 · ND 2 · Culture 2). **Fix:** the red Splat blinks or goes dark, not just red vs green; it also plays a sound.

## System asks

Needs and affordances the games above reveal. Status is what is known now; "unverified" means
not checked against the device code.

| ID | Ask | Games | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | Speaker station: a card reader, or a documented way for wands to control it (play/stop/switch sounds, animal sounds, broadcast when the music stops) | DJ Switch, Musical Colors, Action Echo, Campfire Band | Unverified |
| **S2** | Speaker station: play several sounds at once (layering) | Campfire Band | Unverified |
| **A1** | Who stomped a Splat: assign each child a Splat in a host game, or tap a card to claim the next stomp | Whack-a-Splat (per-child scoring) | Not supported: a Splat knows it was stomped, not by whom |
| **A2** | Stillness check: a wand API for "moved while frozen", separate from counting shakes | Traffic Light, Magic Freeze | Unverified; the accelerometer is there |
| **A3** | Team totals: a leader wand collects scores from many wands and shows the totals | Treasure Hunt Race, Team Charge | Unverified for type 6; the host collects scores in type 9 |
| **A4** | A peer group without a host: one wand passes a token to a random group member | Hot Potato, Secret Agents | Type 7 lobby exists; random pass unverified |
| **A5** | Rhythm relay: a leader's shake timing broadcast to every wand with low enough delay to feel in time | Wizard Beat | Unverified; radio delay may be noticeable |
| **A6** | Wand-to-wand proximity (signal strength) | Little Train, Pass the Secret (dropped) | Not supported; dropped from the list |
| **D1** | Display: a puzzle state and a card sequence or set check | Giant Puzzle, Code Breakers, Recipe Mix-Up | Card reader confirmed; game logic unverified |
| **K1** | Accessibility knowledge: mobility guidance (press Splats by hand, zones instead of running, partner roles) | Most running and stomping games | Missing from `accessibility.md` |
| **K2** | Accessibility knowledge: a vibration motor, if wands have one; `accessibility.md` assumes wand vibration for BLV confirmations | Echo Beat, Magic Freeze, Hot Potato | Unverified |

## Brainstorm prompt additions

For the next batch, add to the equipment rules:

- "A Splat knows it was stomped, not who stomped it."
- The speaker's actual abilities, once S1 is checked.
- "Scores across many wands need a leader wand to collect them."
- "No shaking while running."
- "Text only; no images or links."
- "If the equipment can check it, do not call it honor system."
